summary: Drive many projects at once
recipes: [have-coordination]

A persistent terminal grid on the server, one pane per project, that keeps running when you close
your laptop. You attach to one session from anywhere on the tailnet instead of opening a terminal per
agent.

Adds an `agents` command that attaches to the grid, creating it if it is not already there. Useful on
its own, and independent of whether you have set up the agent sandbox yet.
