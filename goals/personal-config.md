Clones your dotfiles and Claude configuration onto the server, so a new machine does not start with
a stock shell and no settings.

Optional and personal — nothing else here depends on it, and skipping it costs you nothing but the
convenience. Pick it if you are the person whose repos these are; skip it otherwise.

The clone is automatic. Putting the files where they belong is a step you do by hand, because each
repo knows its own layout and the cookbook does not — it stops and tells you exactly what is left.
