name: headless-dongle
summary: An HDMI display emulator — a physical thing you have to own
when: before-you-start
---
Order one now if you do not have it. They are about £10, sold as "HDMI dummy plug",
"headless ghost display adapter", or "display emulator". Get one that matches a resolution
you actually want to screen-share into — 1080p or 1440p.

Why: with no monitor attached, macOS falls back to a tiny framebuffer, and screen sharing
gives you a postage-stamp desktop you cannot work in. The dongle makes the Mac believe a
real display is plugged in.

You need it in hand before the last step, when the monitor comes off. I will tell you the
exact moment to swap them.
